# Sales Insights Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product contribution, and operational efficiency.

## Dashboard Preview
!Screenshot (125).png

## Objectives
- Monitor revenue trends over time
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Analyze geographic sales distribution
- Evaluate customer concentration
- Track order fulfillment performance

## Key Metrics
- Total Revenue: $160.7K
- Total Orders: 273
- Total Quantity Sold: 2.9K
- Total Customers: 5

## Key Insights
- The USA is the largest revenue contributor.
- Electronics and Clothing are the strongest-performing categories.
- A few top products account for a large share of total revenue.
- Revenue is concentrated among a small group of high-value customers.
- Order status data reveals opportunities to improve fulfillment efficiency.
